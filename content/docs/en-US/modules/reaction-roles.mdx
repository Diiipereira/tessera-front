title: Reaction roles
summary: Members pick their own roles, without asking staff.
---

A panel is one message and one row per role a member can pick. Buttons are the default because they are clearer and cannot be removed by accident.

**Permissions:** `Manage Roles`, `Send Messages`, `Add Reactions`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Write the panel">One message, posted in the channel you choose.</Step>
	<Step title="Add the roles">
		One row per role a member can pick, each with an emoji and a label.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose buttons or reactions">
		Buttons are clearer and cannot be removed by accident. Reactions look more familiar.
	</Step>
</Steps>
