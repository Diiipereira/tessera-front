A scheduled message is a text or an embed, a channel, and a time: either one date, or weekdays with
an hour. The clock is the **server's timezone**, not the browser of whoever set it up.

## The hour is local, and that takes work

"Nine in the morning" is not an instant: it is a local time that becomes a different UTC depending
on daylight saving. Tessera works out the instant, measures the timezone offset at that point,
corrects it, and **measures again** — because the first measurement can land on the wrong side of a
shift.

| Situation                        | What happens                            |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| The hour does not exist that day | The message goes out the following hour |
| The hour happens twice           | The message goes out on the **first**   |

That is the convention of cron and RFC 5545. Without it, the day of the shift would send the message
an hour early, which looks like a bug.

If the send fails, that day's message is lost and the reason goes to the log. It is a deliberate
trade.

<Callout tone="warning" title="A message more than an hour late is not sent">
	After a long outage the scheduled time is in the past and the sweep would find it due — posting
	"good morning" at three in the afternoon is worse than not posting. The one-hour window is loose
	enough to survive a restart and tight enough that nothing lands in the wrong part of the day.
</Callout>

The next time is computed from **now**, not from the missed occurrence. That is what stops a daily
message firing twenty-four times in a row to "catch up" on a day of downtime.

## Editing without losing the clock

The whole list is saved at once, but each message is written by its own id. Deleting and reinserting
would lose the last-run date and the position on the clock: renaming a message must not wipe its
history.

A past date is accepted on write — refusing would block saving the list whenever it held a one-off
schedule that had already run. Instead the message shows as **already sent**, and there is no silent
failure.

## When it does not work

| Symptom                           | Likely cause                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The message never goes out        | The module or the message is off, or permissions are missing      |
| It went out an hour early or late | The server timezone is wrong — it lives under Settings            |
| Nothing after the bot was offline | It fell more than an hour behind and was dropped, with a log line |
| The message shows as already sent | Its one-off date has passed                                       |
| One weekday never fires           | It is not ticked — the days are read in the server's timezone     |
