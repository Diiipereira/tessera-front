---
title: Scheduled messages
summary: Post on a timer, without anyone having to remember.
---

A schedule is a message, a channel and a time. Every schedule runs in the server timezone, which is set once in Settings rather than per message.

**Permissions:** `View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Embed Links`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Write the message">Text or an embed, the same composer the other modules use.</Step>
	<Step title="Pick the days and the time">
		Days of the week plus one time of day. The server timezone decides what that time means.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Leave it running">
		A message that missed its slot by more than an hour is skipped rather than posted late.
	</Step>
</Steps>
