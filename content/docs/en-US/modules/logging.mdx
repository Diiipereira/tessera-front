---
title: Logging
summary: Write server events to a channel. Turn on only what you will actually read.
---

Logging writes what happened to a channel you choose. The temptation is to turn everything on; the advice is not to, because a log nobody reads is worse than no log — it looks like coverage.

**Permissions:** `View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Embed Links`, `View Audit Log`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Pick the channel">Make it staff-only. Deleted message content ends up here.</Step>
	<Step title="Choose the events">
		Start with joins, leaves, deletes and moderation actions. Add more when you find yourself
		wishing you had them.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Exclude the noise">
		Ignored channels are absolute — nothing from them is ever logged, whatever the events say.
	</Step>
</Steps>
