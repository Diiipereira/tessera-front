title: Moderation
summary: Warns, timeouts, mutes and bans — every one of them written to the case log.
---

Moderation is the case log plus the commands that write to it. Every punishment opens a case, whether it came from a slash command, the dashboard, or an escalation rule firing on its own.

**Permissions:** `Kick Members`, `Ban Members`, `Moderate Members`, `Manage Roles`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Protect the roles that matter">
		A protected role cannot be actioned by the bot, so a moderator cannot ban an admin by mistake.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Decide what the member is told">
		The DM is sent **before** the punishment lands, so a ban still reaches them.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Set the escalation ladder">
		Points build up per warning, and a threshold applies the punishment you chose. Only warns,
		timeouts and mutes count towards it.
	</Step>
</Steps>
