Every week the Epic Games Store gives one or two games away. With this module on, Tessera posts each of
them in a message of its own, in the channel you pick, under the Tessera Gaming name, with the store
picture, the moment the offer ends and a button to claim it.

Epic usually swaps its free games on Thursdays at 15:00 UTC. The next game is already stored before the
swap, so the announcement joins the queue on the first sweep after it — within a minute. When the week
has two games, the second one goes out five minutes after the first.

The card is always the same: the game name, the blue edge, when the offer ends and the picture. Under
the picture comes "Next up", with the next games and the day they arrive, and under the card the button
that opens the store page. "Next up" only appears once the store has announced what comes next, and it
can be turned off. Discord writes the dates itself, in the time zone and language of whoever reads.

## The Tessera Gaming name

The announcement does not go out under the bot's name: it goes out as **Tessera Gaming**, with Tessera's
picture. To do that, Tessera creates a webhook in the channel the first time it announces, and reuses it
after that. It shows up under Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks.

Creating the webhook needs the **Manage Webhooks** permission in the channel. Without it the
announcement still goes out, only under the bot's name. If someone deletes the webhook, Tessera creates
a new one on the next announcement.

## Who gets notified

Up to five roles, and @everyone when you turn the option on. Each message notifies again: in a week with
two games, that is two notifications, five minutes apart.

For @everyone to notify anyone, Tessera needs the **Mention @everyone, @here, and All Roles** permission
in the channel. Without it the mention shows in the text and nobody gets pinged. The same permission
covers a role that is not marked as mentionable.

## What counts as a free game

Epic mixes free games, ordinary discounts, DLC and bundles in the same list. Tessera announces only what
is actually free:

| Announced                                                 | Left out                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| A game whose final price is zero, inside the offer window | An ordinary discount, of any size   |
| A free bundle                                             | Free DLC for a game you may not own |
| The next free game, under the picture                     | An offer that has not started yet   |

The price is not shown. It changes from country to country, and the server language does not say where
its members are.

## Testing

**Send a test** posts the announcement of the first game that is free right now to the saved channel,
with a line naming who asked. The test notifies nobody: the roles and @everyone show up in the message,
but no one gets pinged. If nothing is free at the moment, the test shows the next game instead. If the
test goes out under the bot's name, the dashboard says so: Tessera is missing Manage Webhooks in the
channel.

## The X that removes the card

Anyone with the Manage Messages permission sees an X over the card, and Discord asks whether to remove
the attachments from the message for everyone. That is Discord's own feature, not Tessera's: the
permission lets them hide the card on any message, from any bot. Members without it do not see the X,
and no bot can turn that button off.

## Options

<Fields module="game-alerts" />

Everything can be set from Discord as well: `/config game-alerts` for the channel, @everyone and "Next
up", and `/config game-alerts edit:` for the roles and the stores.

## When it does not work

| Symptom                                      | Likely cause                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The module will not switch on                | No channel has been picked                                          |
| Nothing was posted on Thursday               | The bot cannot see or write in the channel, or lacks Embed Links    |
| The announcement went out under the bot name | The Manage Webhooks permission is missing in the channel            |
| @everyone did not notify anyone              | The Mention @everyone, @here, and All Roles permission is missing   |
| The test says there is no game               | The store has no free game and has not announced the next one       |
| A game showed up again months later          | The store gave it away again, in a new window — that is a new offer |
