Welcome posts a message the moment someone joins and can hand them a role on the way in. It is
the module most servers turn on first, because it is the one members notice.

**Permissions:** `View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Embed Links`, `Manage Roles`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Pick the channel">
		Where the greeting is posted. The bot needs **Send Messages** there, and **Embed Links** as well
		if you send an embed.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Write the message">
		Plain text or an embed. Both accept the same variables — `{user}` and `{server}`.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Turn it on">
		The module stays off until you enable it, and it cannot be enabled without a channel. Nothing is
		posted while it is off.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Variables

Every message field in this module accepts the same two. That includes each part of an embed —
its title, its description and its fields. Anything the bot cannot resolve is left as written
rather than blanked, so a typo is visible instead of invisible.

| Variable   | Becomes               |
| ---------- | --------------------- |
| `{user}`   | The member who joined |
| `{server}` | The server name       |

Whether `{user}` becomes a real ping or just a name is decided by the module's mention setting,
not by the variable.

<Callout tone="info" title="Ghost mentions are a separate message">
	With the mention set to *ghost*, the bot posts the ping on its own and deletes it a moment later,
	so the member is notified without the greeting carrying a mention. In that mode `{user}` in your
	text becomes the name, not a ping — otherwise the member would be pinged twice and only one of
	them would disappear.
</Callout>
