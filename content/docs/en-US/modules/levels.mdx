---
title: Levels
summary: XP for talking, ranks to show for it, and roles handed out along the way.
---

Levels gives a random amount of XP per message, at most once per cooldown, and hands out roles when a member crosses a level you picked. The randomness and the cooldown together are what stop it from rewarding spam.

**Permissions:** `View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Manage Roles`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Set the XP range">
		A random amount between the minimum and maximum is granted per message, at most once per
		cooldown.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose a curve">
		The curve is how much harder each level is than the one before it. The chart on the module page
		shows what the first levels cost.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Add role rewards">
		Given when a member reaches the level. Roles above the bot in the list cannot be granted.
	</Step>
</Steps>
