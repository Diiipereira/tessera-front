Levels hand out a **random** amount of XP per message, at most once per cooldown, and give roles
when a member crosses a step on the ladder. The randomness and the cooldown together are what stop
the module from rewarding spam.

## The curve

A level costs **`round(curve × level²)`** in total XP. The curve is the only number you touch; the
chart on the screen draws the first fifty levels for whatever value you pick.

| Curve | Level 5 | Level 10 | Level 25 | Level 50 |
| ----- | ------- | -------- | -------- | -------- |
| 50    | 1,250   | 5,000    | 31,250   | 125,000  |
| 100   | 2,500   | 10,000   | 62,500   | 250,000  |
| 200   | 5,000   | 20,000   | 125,000  | 500,000  |

<Callout tone="info" title="Changing the curve rewrites nobody">
	A level is always worked out from the XP and the current curve. Lowering the curve promotes
	everyone at once; raising it demotes. Nothing is recalculated in the database, because it does not
	need to be — the formula is the truth.
</Callout>

The cooldown is per member, per server. Thirty messages in a minute count as **one**, which is why
the number on the Members screen is called _XP messages_ and not _messages_: it was never the total
someone said.

With the cooldown at zero, every message pays.

## Earning XP in voice

A 60-second sweep looks at who is in a voice channel and pays them. It is not a session timer:
holding the join instant in memory would lose everything on a restart, and would never pay someone
who sits in a call for six hours.

A restart costs at most a minute of voice XP, because there is no accumulated state to lose.

## The role ladder

Roles handed out when a member reaches the level. The whole ladder is replaced when you save, and
each step can remove the role from the step below it.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Going down takes; going up gives">
	Zeroing or lowering someone's XP removes the roles above their new level — otherwise `/xp reset`
	would leave the level-40 role on them. What does **not** happen is rebuilding the ladder from the
	bottom: a role replaced by a step above never comes back on the way down, because the table does
	not record which replaced which.
</Callout>

A role above Tessera in the list is never given.

## The level-up announcement

<Message
	content="Nice one, @Lia! You reached level 10."
	caption="The mention notifies whoever levelled up. An @everyone pasted into the text notifies nobody — on a server with 500 active members that would be a ping machine."
/>

The announcement can go out in the channel where the person spoke, or in a fixed channel. With the
text empty, Tessera uses the default sentence in the server's language. Turning on **Announce with
an embed** makes it an embed — edited on the screen, or with
`/config levels edit:Announcement embed` inside Discord.

## Commands

<Commands module="levels" />

`/rank` and `/leaderboard` are for everyone: seeing your own progress is not administration. `/xp`
belongs to whoever has Manage Server, and refuses bots — bots do not level up.

## When it does not work

| Symptom                          | Likely cause                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nobody earns XP                  | The module is off, or the **Message Content** intent is off     |
| One channel never pays           | It is on the exempt channels list                               |
| One member earns nothing         | One of their roles is on the exempt roles list                  |
| The level role is never given    | It sits above Tessera, or **Manage Roles** is missing           |
| Everyone's level changed at once | The curve was edited — a level is always recomputed from the XP |
| Nobody earns voice XP            | Alone in the channel, deafened, or in the AFK channel           |
