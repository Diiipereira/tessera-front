---
title: Giveaways
summary: Timed draws with entry requirements, and a reroll when a winner goes quiet.
---

A giveaway starts the moment you create it and draws when the timer runs out. Requirements are checked when a member enters, and someone who already won is never put back in the hat.

**Permissions:** `View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Embed Links`

## Setting it up

<Steps>
	<Step title="Describe the prize">
		It starts the moment you create it, so write it before you save.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Set the requirements">
		Required roles and a minimum level, checked when the member presses enter.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Draw, or reroll">
		A reroll excludes the previous winners and picks again from everyone still eligible.
	</Step>
</Steps>
