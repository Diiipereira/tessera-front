Tessera is a Discord bot with ten modules and a dashboard for configuring them. Everything the
bot does is off until you turn it on, and every module has its own page here.

<Callout tone="info" title="The reference tables are generated">
	Every option table and command list on these pages is read from the bot's own config registry when
	the page loads. They cannot describe a setting the bot does not have, and they cannot fall behind
	a setting it gained.
</Callout>

## Two doors, one room

A setting changed with a slash command and the same setting changed in the dashboard are **the
same setting**. There is no sync, no import, no "push to Discord" button — both are doors into
one stored config, and whichever you used last is what the bot is doing right now.

That sounds obvious and it is the hardest thing in the product to build. It is also why you can
hand the dashboard to someone who has never used a slash command, and hand slash commands to
someone who never opens a browser, and have them both be right.

## Where to start

<Steps>
	<Step title="Add the bot">
		One OAuth screen. You need **Manage Server** on the Discord server you are adding it to.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Turn on one module">
		Welcome is the usual first one, because it is the one members notice.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Come back for the rest">
		Every module works on its own. Nothing here needs anything else here to be useful.
	</Step>
</Steps>
