---
title: Adding the bot
summary: The OAuth screen, what it asks for, and what to do when the bot is quiet.
---

Adding Tessera is one OAuth screen. Discord asks you which server, and which permissions to
grant; Tessera asks for nothing else.

<Callout tone="info" title="You need Manage Server">
	Discord only lists servers where you hold **Manage Server**. If a server is missing from the
	picker, that permission is why.
</Callout>

## The steps

<Steps>
	<Step title="Press Add to Discord">
		From the landing page, or from the server picker in the dashboard.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Choose the server">Only servers where you have Manage Server appear.</Step>
	<Step title="Leave the permissions alone">
		The defaults are the union of what the modules need. Removing one here does not break the bot —
		the module that needs it simply refuses to save, and says which permission is missing.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Open the dashboard">
		The server appears in the picker within a few seconds of the bot joining.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## The bot joined but does nothing

That is expected. Every module is off until you turn it on. A freshly added bot sits in the
member list and waits.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Role position matters more than permissions">
	A bot cannot act on a member whose highest role sits above its own, whatever permissions it has.
	If commands work on regular members and fail on moderators, drag the Tessera role up.
</Callout>
