Adding Tessera is one OAuth screen. Discord asks which server and which permissions to grant;
Tessera asks for nothing else.

## The steps

<Steps>
	<Step title="Press Add to Discord">
		From the home page, or from the server picker inside the dashboard.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Pick the server">
		Only servers where you have **Manage Server** are listed. If a server is missing, that
		permission is why.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Leave the permissions alone">
		The defaults are the union of what the modules need. Removing one here does not break the bot —
		the module that needs it refuses to save, and says which one is missing.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Drag the role up">
		Server Settings → Roles. Tessera's role has to sit **above** every role it will hand out or
		manage. It is the step most people skip.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Open the dashboard">
		The server shows up in the picker a few seconds after the bot joins.
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Role position beats permission

A bot never acts on a member whose highest role sits above its own, and never hands out a role above
its own — whatever permissions it holds. No Discord permission overrides that.

```text
Owner
Admin
Tessera          ← everything below this line can be managed and handed out
Moderator
Member
Muted
```

<Callout tone="danger" title="Administrator is not a shortcut">
	Granting Administrator does **not** lift the hierarchy rule, and hands over far more than any
	module uses. Position the role instead.
</Callout>

If the commands work on ordinary members and fail on moderators, this is it — and it is the most
common report of all.

## The bot joined and does nothing

That is expected. Every module starts off until you turn it on; a freshly added bot sits in the
member list and waits.

## Removing the bot

From Discord, kick it like any member. From the dashboard, it is the button under **Settings**.

<Callout tone="info" title="Leaving does not erase the configuration">
	The server's row is kept, and adding the bot back restores everything as it was — modules on,
	texts, roles, panels. A 30-day grace period is what actually deletes it.
</Callout>
