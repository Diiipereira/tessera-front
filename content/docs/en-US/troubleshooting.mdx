---
title: Troubleshooting
summary: The failures that come up most, and what actually causes them.
---

Nearly every report falls into one of these. They are ordered by how often they turn out to be
the answer.

## The bot does nothing

1. The module is off. Every module ships off and stays off until you turn it on.
2. The bot cannot see the channel. A channel the bot cannot view is locked in every picker — if
   yours is selectable, this is not it.
3. The Tessera role sits below the member you are acting on. Position beats permission, always.
4. The command is hidden from that member. `/config` and the administrative commands are
   restricted by Discord itself, under Server Settings → Integrations.

## Roles are not being granted

A bot can only grant roles **below its own highest role**. Roles above it are locked in the
picker for exactly this reason, so if the role was selectable and still is not granted, check
whether it moved since.

## A slash command does not appear in Discord

Newly registered command names can take a minute to propagate, and Discord caches them per
client. Reconnecting the Discord client is faster than waiting.

## A save was refused

Someone else saved the same module while your page was open. Reload and look at the audit log —
it shows who, when, and what they changed — then make your change again on top of theirs.

## A command ran and the answer was a refusal

That is the bot working. A refusal — role hierarchy, a protected role, a bot as the target — is
answered with a readable message and counts as a successful run on the **Commands** page. What
that page counts as a failure is the bot breaking, not a moderator being told no.
